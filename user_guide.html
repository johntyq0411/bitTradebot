Freqtrade BTC/USDT Bot — User Guide

✓ PAPER TRADING ONLY Freqtrade 2026.7 Python 3.11 BTC/USDT · 1h

This is an automated cryptocurrency paper-trading bot built on Freqtrade, an open-source trading framework. It runs an Advanced Machine Learning (FreqAI) strategy on the BTC/USDT pair using 1-hour candles, with no real money at risk — all trades are fully simulated.

⚠ Safety First dry_run: true is enforced in all configs. Exchange API keys are blank. The bot cannot place real orders. Do not add real API keys unless you fully understand the risks and have thoroughly validated the strategy.

Machine Learning Architecture (FreqAI)

From Multi-Factor Inputs to Predictive Output

Unlike traditional bots that rely on rigid, human-coded rules (e.g., "Buy if RSI is below 30"), this system operates a LightGBM Neural Network. Every hour, the AI ingests 38 distinct features across two different timeframes (1-hour for precision, 4-hour for the macro trend), weighs them against its historical training, and calculates a unified probability score.

Continuous Learning
The bot retrains its models dynamically. As market regimes shift from bullish to bearish, the AI automatically adapts its internal logic without requiring human code changes.
Multi-Timeframe Analysis
The bot executes trades on the 1-hour chart to target 3-5% swings, but it simultaneously feeds 4-hour chart data into the AI so it never loses sight of the overarching macro trend.

How the AI is Trained

The AI does not look at the market like a human. It looks at historical data, tries to find mathematical correlations, and tests itself.

The Sliding Window Training

We use a 30-day sliding window. To trade on August 1st, the AI looks at the data from July 1st to July 30th, trains a custom model for the current market regime, and then trades on August 1st. Tomorrow, the window slides forward by one day, and it retrains.

Training Data Sensitivity (180 vs 365 Days)

During our research, we tested two different historical dataset sizes to seed the bot:

  • 180-Day Dataset (The Sniper): Produced an 87% win rate with only 8 trades. Because the last 180 days were largely a choppy/bullish regime, the AI learned to be extremely selective and only buy deep, high-probability dips.
  • 365-Day Dataset (The Active Trader): Produced an 81% win rate with 16 trades. Because a full year includes multiple violent bull and bear phases, the AI learned broader patterns. It traded twice as often, but took slightly more losses as a result of being more aggressive.

Conclusion: We locked in the 180-Day dataset because we prioritize capital preservation and a high win rate over trade frequency.

Entry & Exit Logic

▲ BUY (Enter Long)

Controlled purely by the Neural Network:

  • AI Prediction: The LightGBM model predicts that the price will rise by more than 1.0% over the next 3 candles (3 hours).

▼ SELL (Exit Long)

ANY condition triggers exit:

  • AI Prediction: The AI model detects a reversal and predicts the price will drop by more than 1.0% over the next 3 candles.
  • ROI target hit (aggressively taking partial gains)
  • Hard Stop-loss at −5% triggered (Catastrophic protection)

Risk Management Rules

Minimal ROI Table

The bot auto-sells if profit reaches these thresholds (measured from trade open):

Time in TradeRequired Profit to ExitLogic
0 minutes (any time)5.0%Take 5% immediately if it spikes
2 hours +3.0%Accept lower target after 2h hold
4 hours +1.5%Just get out profitably after 4h

Stop-Loss

A hard stop at −4% from entry price. If BTC price drops 4% after entry, the position is closed immediately at market price, limiting the loss.

ℹ Example Trade Entry at $100,000. Stop-loss fires at $96,000 (−4%). ROI target (5%) fires at $105,000. The trade will exit whichever comes first — ROI, stoploss, or signal.

What We Learned: AI vs Hard Rules

During testing, we discovered a crucial lesson about mixing Machine Learning (LightGBM) with traditional hard-coded trading rules (like Trailing Stops and Dynamic ATR).

⚠ The "Choked Edge" Trap

When we added a traditional trailing stop (locking in 1% profit) and tightened the stop-loss using a dynamic ATR (1.5%-2%), the AI's win rate crashed from 88% down to 39%.

Why did this happen?

  • The AI needs room to breathe: Our model was trained to predict price movements 3 hours into the future. By suffocating it with tight trailing stops, the bot exited winning trades on minor 15-minute noise, preventing the 3-hour prediction from coming true.
  • Conflicting Mandates: The AI decides when to buy and sell based on 38 factors (DXY, Funding Rates, RSI). When you slap a hard rule on top of it (e.g. "always sell if it pulls back 0.5% from peak"), you are overriding a sophisticated multi-factor neural network with a dumb, rigid rule.
✓ The Solution

Let the AI run completely naked. It calculates its own probability scores. We only use a single, wide catastrophic stop-loss (e.g. -5%) to protect the account against flash crashes, leaving the actual entry/exit timing entirely to the FreqAI engine.

⚠ On Synthetic Data This backtest used GBM-generated data with σ=80% annual volatility — more chaotic than real BTC price movement. Real BTC has trend persistence and support/resistance structure that the EMA crossover exploits well. The −24% result on synthetic data is a stress-test, not a forecast.

The Core Problem: Loss/Win Asymmetry

Average winner (+1.93%) is smaller than average loser (−3.29%). This means even with a 60.7% win rate, the losses outweigh the wins. The fix is either:

  • Raise ROI targets — let winners run to 8–10% instead of 5%
  • Tighten the stop-loss — e.g. −2.5% instead of −4% (but risks more stopped trades)
  • Use a trailing stop — locks in gains as price rises

Best Practices — Safety

🔴 Never do these things
