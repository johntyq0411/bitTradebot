Regime-Gated Trend Strategy (BTC/USDT)

1. The Strategy Mandate & Rationale

The objective of this strategy is to harvest the trend-following risk premium in the cryptocurrency market. Cryptocurrency exhibits severe, prolonged drawdown periods (crypto winters) followed by violent momentum-driven expansion cycles.

Economic Rationale: Rather than attempting to predict price direction using complex Machine Learning (which our audits proved possessed zero correlation to future returns), this strategy capitalizes on momentum inertia. It mathematically ensures capital preservation during macro drawdowns (the "Bear Regime") and aggressively remains exposed during structural uptrends.

Readings:

  • ADX is the most sensitive parameter. ADX 20 → +61.25%, ADX 15 → +57.08%, but ADX 25 → +32.19%. The trend-strength gate is doing real work; tightening it halves the return.

We deliberately do not re-optimize toward that peak: the difference is well within Monte Carlo sampling noise (bootstrap p5–p95 spanned −10% to +220%), and chasing it would be the overfitting this gate exists to prevent.

6. Tail Risk & "Kill Switch" Caveats

The risk committee must be aware of the following structural vulnerabilities before deploying capital:

Gap Risk (The Flash Crash)

Because the primary risk management is a structural moving average exit (SMA200) rather than a tight trailing stop, a sudden flash crash (e.g., a 20% drop in a single 1-hour candle) will bypass the moving average before the candle closes.

Mitigation: A hard -15% disaster stop has been implemented in the strategy file. However, this stop never fired during the 2-year backtest (the worst trade was -6.34%). It is entirely untested theoretical insurance.

  • Statistical Thinness: With only 88 trades over 2 years, the p-value is 0.32. This is directional evidence of an edge, not statistical proof. Hundreds of trades over a longer out-of-sample period are required for high confidence.
  • Counterparty Risk: The bot relies on 100% uptime from the Binance API. Network disconnection during a structural exit trigger will leave the portfolio exposed.
  • Defensive Opportunity Cost: The strategy is designed for capital preservation first. In a massive, vertical bull market, it will underperform an aggressive leveraged long position.
